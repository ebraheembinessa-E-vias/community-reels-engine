Assistant: the faceless route.

The person may arrive with no recording and only an idea.
Use docs/faceless.md. Turn the interview into a brief and concise script: opening, useful body and one next action. Present a plain-language scene plan alongside it. Verify factual claims. Get script approval before narration or paid assets.
Ask for a voice choice: their own recording, an authorized artificial narrator, or intentional silence. Use a capability actually available in their environment. Google TTS is separate from video analysis. Preview generated speech and verify every word. Do not silently replace a provider or clone a real person's voice.
Measure actual narration and caption intervals; do not estimate onsets from word counts. For text-only, review reading time. Write the story plan yourself, with actual script approval and purposeful visuals. story imports narration or prepares silence; it does not generate a voice.
python3 reel.py story work/story.json --name my-story --audio work/voice.wav
# Use --silent instead of --audio only when intentionally text-only.
# After the person reviews and approves the master and captions:
python3 reel.py approve my-story --by "Creator name"
python3 reel.py dress my-story --style diagram --faceless
python3 reel.py render my-story --style diagram --faceless
The plain-background story master is an audio/text checkpoint before animation. Show it with its captions; do not describe it as a finished reel. Review the final animation at normal speed and fix empty visual gaps or mismatched narration.
